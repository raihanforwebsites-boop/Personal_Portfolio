01 About

About

Raihan is a final-year BA Economics Honours student at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bannerghatta Road Campus, Bangalore.

His research paper on fisheries livelihood vulnerability in coastal Maharashtra is indexed in Scopus and Web of Science, and has been presented at international conferences. He holds the Yale Financial Markets certificate, completed with Honors.

His work sits at the intersection of economic research, applied data analysis, and business problems — with a focus on financial research, business analytics, strategy, and founder's-office work. He is working toward a postgraduate management programme (PGP/MBA) over the next two to three years, alongside building toward independent ventures.
